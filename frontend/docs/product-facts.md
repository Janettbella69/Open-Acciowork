# OPHIRA AI product facts
> Verified: 2026-08-05

## Existence and status
- OPHIRA AI is an in-development private GitHub project owned by `Janettbella69`.
- The repository currently contains the brand foundation only; no released frontend or public product UI existed before this implementation.
- Default branch: `main`.

## Product definition
- Audience: small cross-border businesses and compact teams.
- Purpose: connect global demand, products and supply, supply chains, customers, and transaction opportunities.
- Product journey: market opportunity → supply fit → customer path → real transaction.
- Brand proposition: “Discover what the world needs. Connect who can supply it.”
